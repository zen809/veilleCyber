### [HIGH] [CTO of ‘RansomwareHelp’ Indicted for Wire Fraud](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/11/cto-of-ransomwarehelp-indicted-for-wire-fraud/)
**Source:** Krebs on Security | 2026-11-21
**Thème:** Ransomware

The CTO and co-founder of ransomwarehelp.com has been indicted for wire fraud after an FBI sting operation revealed the company was not using advanced cryptographic tools to decrypt data, but was simply paying the ransom to attackers and charging victims a markup. This case exposes fraudulent practices in the ransomware recovery industry, which is critical knowledge for incident response planning and vendor risk management in the healthcare sector.
