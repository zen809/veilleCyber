### [MEDIUM] [pCloud Password Manager Hit by Cyber Attack Involving Email Address Exposure and Login Attempts](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/pcloud-password-manager-hit-by-cyber.html)
**Source:** Thehackernews | 2026-11-28
**Thème:** Incidents

pCloud Pass, a cross-platform password manager developed by the Swiss company pCloud, confirmed it was targeted by a cyber attack that resulted in the exposure of a small number of user email addresses. Users reported seeing unknown devices connected to their accounts, prompting the company to state that the attack was promptly contained.
